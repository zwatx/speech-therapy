Approved after correction: the first clinician-to-learner assignment slice now makes the server—not the child client—the authority for consent, content, dose, retry, and completion state.

Context

This review covers the bounded foundation exercise assignment added to the clinician portal, Postgres persistence layer, device API, and iOS learner app. The release posture remains candidate-silent-only: camera analysis and learner-facing corrective feedback are hard disabled, and this slice is not approved for clinical or production use.

The critical boundary is continuity across devices, retries, network ambiguity, and app restarts. A child client cannot be trusted to declare which repetition comes next or whether the prescribed dose is complete.

Verdict

Approve the local vertical slice. The first independent review correctly blocked the implementation because retry exhaustion could appear as success and progress lived only in client memory. The corrected flow persists attempt evidence, derives the next legal slot on the server, exposes explicit ready/retry-limit/completed states, and requires the iOS app to refresh after any ambiguous upload outcome. The follow-up review found no blocking issues.

Release boundary and next questions
