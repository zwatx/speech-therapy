# Fixed rating-0 proper-loss experiment

Declared September 27, 2026, before new fitting or outcome inspection. This is a development experiment on the same children and prompts used in several previous comparisons, not independent confirmation. The hypothesis is that giving the rare rating-0 event a second *proper* log-loss term improves rating-0 discrimination without losing the current final-feature head's probability quality or consistency. Failure closes this exact recipe as an automatic replacement; success warrants a separate participant-level confirmation study, not learner use.

## One fixed training change

For each position, let `q=(q0,q1,q2)` be its five-vote fractions and `p=softmax(z)` the head probabilities. Optimize the mean over positions of

`L = -sum_k qk log(pk) - q0 log(p0) - (1-q0) log(1-p0)`.

The added binary term has coefficient **1**, with no coefficient search or class-prior correction. First compute `logp=log_softmax(z)`, then calculate `log(1-p0)=logsumexp(logp1,logp2)` and use `logp0` directly. This preserves common-logit-shift invariance even with extreme float32 logits; never form `1-p0` then take a logarithm. Mixed votes remain fractional; no hard rating-0 labels, duplicate samples or new annotations. Both terms are proper losses for the same population distribution, but finite-data calibration and ranking may still worsen. This changes the loss, not model capacity or target meaning.

Otherwise preserve the historical recipe: hidden width 128, dropout 0.1, AdamW learning rate 0.0003 and weight decay 0.01, batch size 64, gradient-norm cap 5, maximum 100 inner epochs, patience 15 and improvement tolerance `1e-7`. Select the earliest improving epoch by **ordinary unweighted five-vote categorical NLL** on the same inner-selection rows, not the composite training loss or an AP endpoint. Epoch zero is ineligible, as in the historical recipe. Fresh refits use the selected epoch count. Retain the original deterministic row order, RNG and training metadata. Identify this as a distinct coefficient-1 training objective in config, checkpoint, replay and validation, while retaining the original unweighted prediction and selection metrics. Compute and save ordinary NLL and both loss terms during training so the objective can be audited.
