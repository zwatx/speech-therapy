# UltraSuite clinician-rated recording acquisition

The 518 recordings referenced by the acquired UXSSD and UPX rating metadata are now available locally: 496 UXSSD recordings and 22 UPX recordings. All were acquired, including the child below the target age range, shared controls and repeated-rating items. No score-based selection occurred. The provider makes these resources available for noncommercial research under CC BY-NC 4.0; attribution and the provider's documentation remain attached. [UltraSuite downloads](https://ultrasuite.github.io/download/), [UXSSD](https://ultrasuite.github.io/data/uxssd/), [UPX](https://ultrasuite.github.io/data/upx/).

The acquisition contains 216,285,448 bytes and 4,903.915 seconds of audio, approximately 82 minutes. Every waveform is mono, uncompressed 16-bit PCM at 22,050 Hz. Recording durations range from 1.811 to 39.845 seconds. This describes recording format and availability, not pronunciation quality or model accuracy.

Two exact filename whitelists transferred only the listed WAV files. Every local file matched its published listing size; the acquired set has no missing or additional files. Each original waveform now has a local SHA-256 pin. A standard-library WAV reader checked all headers and decoded frame lengths. A separate RIFF/chunk parser independently reconstructed all 518 container sizes, PCM formats, block alignments and frame counts and agreed with those checks. The provider did not supply content hashes, so listing-size agreement and local pins do not authenticate remote contents.

Evidence is preserved in `work/ultrasuite-rated-audio-20260928/`: the prospective acquisition plan, both transfer logs, `acquisition-manifest.json` and `root-independent-verification.json`. The acquisition plan SHA-256 is `b555c46bc4bdd07aa26b52413ae18b49c2953de5edc76fd5a724adda5a174085`; the manifest SHA-256 is `c7ff6a1339940c0c3fed18dd4e7540e69bde1c34444a5be5fcd2da14753f1cf8`.

No ultrasound, signal features, predictions, training, uploads or Colab allocation occurred. The frozen text/metadata audit reads none of these waveforms and keeps its own acquisition flags scoped to that audit. The original recording bytes are preserved; no resampling, trimming or repair has occurred.

The current research feature adapter requires 16 kHz PCM and bounds recordings to 30 seconds, so these originals cannot be passed into it unchanged. A separate, declared adapter must specify resampling and handling of longer recordings. Provider SAMPA pronunciations also require an explicit conversion to the model's canonical notation, preserving alternative pronunciations and target occurrences. Word-boundary linkage, rating-scale compatibility and independent model evaluation remain separate requirements. Neither this acquisition nor the clinician ratings establish produced-phone identity, clinical correctness, all-23-consonant coverage or performance for ages 11–12.
