# Fixed NISQA training-side ingestion pilot

Declared September 27, 2026, before archive-member acquisition, rating inspection, playback or model inference. This is an ingestion and supervision-feasibility audit, not a model-quality experiment. It adds real recording-quality labels to the research inventory; it does not supply child pronunciation, produced-phone, speech-presence or clinical labels. The retained pronunciation package stays in place.

## Source, rights and information boundary

Use only the official NISQA v1 record [4728081](https://zenodo.org/records/4728081), archive `https://zenodo.org/records/4728081/files/NISQA_Corpus.zip`. The advertised archive size is 15,945,499,507 bytes and MD5 is `c4997a873e9397dd6d1fa787d70c3106`. Partial retrieval cannot verify the archive-wide MD5. Preserve official source documents and the applicable notices extracted from the archive, alongside response validators, member metadata, CRC/size checks and local SHA-256 hashes. The [author's LIVE description](https://github.com/gabrielmittag/NISQA/wiki/NISQA-Corpus#nisqa_train_live-and-nisqa_val_live) permits research under the original source terms; check the actual subset notices before audio acquisition.

The intended subset is **NISQA_TRAIN_LIVE** only. The author documents 1,020 files from 486 source speakers, using LibriVox material replayed through phone or Skype capture. Age 6–12 coverage is not established. Whole-recording quality ratings cannot be transferred to short consonant crops or used as pronunciation judgments.

Official public documentation and member names may be inspected for schema, rights and routing. Do not acquire validation/test audio. If the official label table combines splits, its raw bytes may be acquired and CSV strings tokenized for routing. Do not interpret evaluation targets numerically, expose them in logs/previews/artifacts, compute their statistics or use them for any decision. Do not claim the combined object was literally unopened. Keep raw combined metadata out of human previews.

## Staged, bounded acquisition

1. Freeze the directory-discovery source, protocol, archive identity and runtime declaration; obtain independent implementation review before real archive-range retrieval. Bootstrap with HEAD, then inspect ZIP end records and the central directory. Save the exact member-name/offset/size/CRC inventory. No member is decompressed or inspected in this stage. A ZIP tail range can contain adjacent uninterpreted compressed fragments; record the requested byte ranges rather than claiming those bytes were never transferred.
2. From that inventory, freeze an explicit metadata-member request list before retrieval. Eligible members are root or TRAIN_LIVE notices and the official combined/per-file metadata needed for schema and source identity. Other subset notices may be identified by name but are not imported. The list must give exact names, roles and size bounds, and must exclude audio. Preserve each retrieved member's original bytes and hashes.
3. Inspect notice text, CSV headers and split/path/source identifiers only. Verify TRAIN_LIVE path conventions, the source-reader and capture-condition semantics, rating columns/units/direction/aggregation/missingness, and any uncertainty/vote-count fields using publisher evidence. Do not infer a reader merely from a plausible filename token. If a necessary semantic fact is missing, report it and gather official identifier documentation; do not silently invent it.
4. Freeze exactly 20 TRAIN_LIVE identities before target inspection or audio retrieval: ten phone-path and ten Skype-path files from 20 distinct verified reader groups. Select phone then Skype groups without replacement, ordering groups exclusive to that condition before shared groups so the other quota remains feasible. Within those strata, sort by SHA-256 of salt `cadence-nisqa-train-live-ingest-v1`, NUL, namespace `group`, NUL and the reader identifier, with the identifier as tie breaker. Choose each group's file by the same hash rule using namespace `file` and the canonical path. Preserve the complete eligible identity inventory and reasons for exclusions. Insufficient verified groups/quotas fail selection readiness; do not substitute a different sample after seeing targets.
5. Decode the five quality dimensions only for those frozen 20 training rows. Acquire exactly their allowlisted audio members, then perform the waveform/identity/label audit below. Preserve partial failures and missing selected items; no outcome-based replacement or silent restart.

Metadata discovery has a cumulative **16 MiB** transfer limit; the complete pilot has a cumulative **256 MiB** limit, including failed attempts. Each metadata member has an **8 MiB** compressed/uncompressed bound, accommodating the official combined table's documented size while retaining the cumulative cap. Use exact HTTP byte ranges, HTTPS and the frozen official host. Require HTTP 206, exact Content-Range/total/length, identity encoding and stable object validators across requests. Reject a 200 full-archive fallback before reading its body. Use finite timeouts and bounded retries. Audit ZIP64 offsets, member paths/types/compression, compressed/uncompressed bounds, CRC and size; never extract arbitrary paths or follow archive links. A transport/schema correction receives a separately versioned declaration and review; existing artifacts remain intact.

## Two-pass metadata router

Pass one handles only the header, dataset/split, canonical relative degraded-file path and explicit verified source-identity fields. Require exact TRAIN_LIVE dataset/path agreement; reject unsafe paths, duplicates and inconsistent identities. Numeric quality fields are prohibited as identity/selection inputs. Evaluation split/identifier metadata may support overlap checks without accessing evaluation targets.

Pass two rejects every row outside the frozen 20 training identities **before** accessing quality fields or converting numbers. Parse only those selected rows; require a unique complete join. Preserve source-declared missingness rather than inventing a score. Numeric ranges and direction must come from checked publisher evidence, not a default assumption that a high noisiness value means more physical noise.

Before real CSV routing, tests must cover mixed row order, quoted commas/newlines, path-prefix tricks, missing/duplicate selected IDs, target-column misuse, and malformed/sentinel held-out target strings. Held-out numeric defects must not cause target parsing, and no held-out sentinel may appear in emitted artifacts or logs. Test deterministic quotas and reader-group separation. Before real transport, tests must cover wrong ranges/200 fallback, validator drift, budget/truncation, unsafe members, ZIP64, CRC and allowlist failures.

## Acceptance endpoints

- Exactly 20 fixed training audio members, with original bytes, CRC/size/SHA-256 and complete request accounting; zero evaluation audio acquired and zero evaluation numeric targets exposed or used.
- 20 unique metadata/audio joins and 20 distinct verified reader groups, with source-utterance/book/chapter/capture lineage retained. Phone/Skype versions are not independent speakers. Report broader prompt/book overlap separately.
- Deterministic finite waveform decoding, valid headers/sample rates/channels/frame counts and decoded durations. Record clipping, silence and amplitude descriptively without excluding poor-quality items or normalizing away loudness. Preserve original audio. Any later conversion needs its own fixed recipe and derived hashes.
- Five documented quality dimensions joined to each selected file, with verified scales/direction/aggregation and honest missingness. Incomplete semantics or targets fail readiness for subsequent supervised training.
- Independent post-ingestion review of scope, identity, byte provenance, labels and waveform checks. Passing means the ingestion is ready for a separately fixed larger training study; it does not establish prediction accuracy, a useful rejection threshold or target-age applicability.

No fitting, calibration, pretrained-quality performance assessment, GPU allocation, learner feedback or clinical promotion occurs in this pilot. NISQA pretrained weights may have seen TRAIN_LIVE and cannot turn this pilot into an unseen-data performance test. Qualified target-age production and usable/unusable capture references remain separate evidence dependencies.
