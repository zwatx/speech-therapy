# NISQA selected audio and target ingestion pilot v1

Declared before reading the selected ratings or acquiring audio. This stage extends the preserved discovery and source-code selection protocols, using their already frozen 20 TRAIN_LIVE identities: 10 phone and 10 Skype from 20 approximate publisher source-code groups. No outcome may change selection. These groups do not establish 20 distinct people or speaker-independent evaluation.

The author's thesis, preserved at `work/nisqa-ingest-20260927/thesis-evidence/mittag-thesis.pdf`, printed pages 12–13 and 42, establishes nominal MOS 1–5 for overall quality and the four dimensions, all higher is better. Loudness means suitability, not physical amplitude. The independent scale-method review is preserved alongside it. This stage adopts a strict inclusive finite [1,5] audit for `mos`, `noi`, `dis`, `col`, and `loud`. Rounded continuous-scale conversion could produce slight nominal overshoots: an out-of-range value fails this policy, without claiming the publisher is corrupt. Do not clip, rescale, widen bounds or replace items after seeing values.

Missing tokens are exactly the empty string and literal `NaN`. This is our conservative import policy, not a claim about the complete publisher serialization vocabulary. Do not trim whitespace or change case. Other values must match ASCII `[+-]?(?:[0-9]+(?:\.[0-9]*)?|\.[0-9]+)(?:[eE][+-]?[0-9]+)?` before finite numeric conversion. Unknown, malformed, nonfinite or out-of-range selected values fail and preserve the sample. Missing values remain explicit and make complete-target training readiness false. Read identity columns and skip every unselected row before indexing any rating, vote or standard-deviation column. Raw combined CSV tokenization necessarily includes other splits' strings; their numeric targets are never converted or exposed.

Acquire only the exact 20 frozen degraded audio member names from the official archive with the existing RemoteZipV2 persistent ledger. Preserve cumulative spending, 16 MiB metadata and 256 MiB total body limits, 64 MiB compressed/128 MiB decompressed member caps, unchanged archive identity, allowed host and If-Range validation, CRC and SHA checks. Reopen the directory once in one session, then acquire all selected members with audio payload accounting; local headers still count as metadata. No archive-wide download fallback, new ledger, implicit restart or replacement. Preserve each completed acquisition immediately and all partial/failure evidence.

Audit original waveform bytes without resampling, gain normalization, trimming or output-dependent exclusion. Decoder supports little-endian RIFF PCM 8/16/24/32 bits, IEEE float 32/64 bits, and extensible PCM/float with canonical subtype GUID and valid-bit semantics. Reject unsupported, truncated, duplicate required chunks, inconsistent headers, empty data, nonfinite samples, missing odd-chunk padding, or RIFF declared size differing from the actual file size. Unknown chunks and data before format are allowed. Bound input at 128 MiB, channels at 64, sample rate at 768000 Hz. Integer conversion to digital full-scale float is representation decoding, not gain normalization.

Record format, frames, duration, channel count, sample rate, decoded little-endian float64 hash, peak, RMS, exact-zero fraction and fraction with absolute amplitude at most 0.0001. Record PCM valid-bit rail fraction or floating absolute-amplitude-at-least-one fraction as digital full-scale diagnostics. These are descriptive measurements, not speech-presence labels, clipping perception, quality thresholds or rejection decisions. Finite floating samples outside [-1,1] remain in the audit. Keep channels and every selected failure.

Independent source review precedes selected target conversion and audio requests. Freeze sources, protocol, runtime, exact selection, directory entries, CSV, primary evidence and previous transport snapshot. Separate immutable target, acquisition and waveform outputs; verify original sources again at every phase. Target audit failure does not block safe audio ingestion; audio failure preserves all already completed members. Decoder failure is recorded for that item while the audit continues for other acquired items. Successful ingestion requires exact joins and all 20 acquired/decoded files; complete-target readiness additionally requires all five values on every item. Neither success establishes model gain, child-domain validity or a usable nonspeech threshold. No training, inference, GPU allocation or learner judgments in this stage.
