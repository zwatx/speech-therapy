# Independent sound-reference access — October 6, 2026

No new independent human BR-to-BW validation recordings were acquired in this access check. Do not promote the experimental full-recording sound preference into individualized correction on this evidence.

The [SpeechOcean author documentation](https://github.com/jimbozhang/speechocean762#the-mispronunciations-block) describes selective `canonical-phone`, `index` and `pronounced-phone` notes at poorly rated positions. Approximate labels such as R* and unknown identities are not exact substitute labels; `<unk>` is not a deletion marker. Missing notes and high accuracy ratings do not establish preserved-R ground truth. Five independent expert accuracy ratings do not, on their own, establish five independent realized-phone annotations. The extra notes' annotator/adjudication provenance remains insufficiently specified for that claim.

This is already known project evidence, not a new data breakthrough: the existing [direct-note diagnostic](../model/multiphone-note-likelihood-report.md) explicitly excludes missing-note controls and cannot measure false-positive detection or clinical/generalization accuracy. It was not rerun, refitted or reopened in this check.

Mirror conversion history establishes file conversion, not the original notes' independent human-review provenance.
