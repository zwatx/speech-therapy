beside each word's first review and recheck. A separate Debug-simulator sheet makes

## Ordinary app behavior

Home → **Review, practice, recheck** retains the original five-word order. The
summary joins practice visits by canonical entry ID, even when the chosen practice
order differs. **Practice** uses the existing state wording for saved completion,
unconfirmed saves, skipped and unreached visits; words outside the chosen practice
set say **Not selected**. The frozen first-review and recheck rows are unchanged.
Recording eligibility, save retries, history writes and coordinator transitions
are unchanged. The summary explicitly describes participation, not sound improvement.

## Internal research inspection

With the existing guided research configuration enabled in a Debug simulator,
**Research details** is reachable from practice selection and the completed summary.
It shows First review and Recheck separately, with explicit unavailable states and
the disclaimer **Internal research output. Not validated pronunciation feedback.**
Each target retains its ordered corpus-rating 0/1/2 probabilities. Six decimal
places are display rounding; the retained numeric payload is unchanged. Model,
manifest, declared age, operation, take and visit appear in collapsed provenance.
Different model/age/target contracts receive a non-comparability notice. There is
no winning class, score, delta, recommendation, export or result persistence.

Resolution requires the current open workflow, canonical entry, finished review
round, settled matching first-submission visit, operation and take, plus the retained
admitted typed result. Wrong identities, cancelled/cleanup-blocked outcomes,
legacy timing-only results and malformed target geometry do not produce numbers.
The recognizer's earlier independent request/audio-hash admission remains the trust
boundary: this presentation does not re-prove hashes against the result's own
context or reopen audio. Opening/dismissing details starts no processing.
Repeat creates a new first-review round; prior distributions cannot populate a
fresh skipped or unreached row. Release and physical-device compilation exclude
the result-bearing presentation and controls.
