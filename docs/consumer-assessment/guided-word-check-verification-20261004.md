# Guided word check — October 4, 2026

The browser extension adds an explicit 13-word first round, a complete observation summary, optional manual practice, and one fresh 13-word recheck with paired observations. Free practice keeps all 15 sound-position prompts. This is whole-word recognition and recording feedback; independent target-sound correction, child accuracy, improvement and diagnosis are not established. The broader product goal remains active.

## Behavior

Choose Guided word check, select age6–12, enable checking, and start. Starting requests no microphone, camera or processing. Each Record creates an attempt; each Check explicitly submits that take once and closes the word for the round, including failure or cancellation. Skipped and unreached words remain in the fixed13-word roster. Summary categories total13; operational counts are separately available under How this round was checked.

Practice recordings do not replace first-round observations. Recheck starts a separate round with new recording attempts and the same setup. All13 paired rows remain visible, including No comparison where one side is incomplete. Identical audio bytes are explicitly marked and never treated as independent evidence. Neither differences nor matching observations measure improvement or ability.

Only minimal typed session metadata stays in tab memory. Moving between words/views clears playable clips and their URLs. Ending, switching to free practice, settings/context changes, hidden-page and pagehide handlers clear session state and result-bearing DOM. Mirror capture stops on views where it is hidden and on exit. Server cleanup failure retains its separate processing latch; clearing a tab does not promise erasure of a failed/crashed service copy. No raw transcript, posterior matrix, audio, identity or CSRF token is stored in guided result metadata.
