# Finished-product professional review handoff

The owner delegates engineering content and technical choices to models and defers professional review until the product is finished. This handoff prepares that final review; it does not initiate outreach or reinstate rolling professional approvals during development.

Review the working desktop journey, model engineering decisions, thirteen original illustrations and the actual licensed public reference pack together. The engineering journey permits manual neutral practice when an assessment is unavailable. No probability is a speech score, no word match establishes a target sound's correctness, and no participation count or identical-byte replay demonstrates improvement. Unknown source speaker ages and unverified pronunciation-variant coverage remain visible.

The review package includes the exact fifteen prompt IDs, seventeen dictionary variants and target indexes; chosen versus retained repeated-N occurrences in banana; explicit unsupported ZH in treasure; reference author, variety, source revision, license, conversion and byte bindings; actual negative-input failures and admission challenge results; permission/retention/withdrawal and all-attempt behavior; and the fixed prospective intended-recording admission protocol. Preserve the existing handoff and controlled records rather than creating a competing signoff process.

A later professional review should make factual, version-bound records for:

- Suitability and intended age/readiness/English-variety scope of the words, illustrations, presentation and example audio; acceptable variants and target observability.
- Understandability and safety of the neutral instructions, adult support, unavailable explanations and practice/replay flow. Any individualized corrective cue requires its own approved task and evidence; none is inferred here.
- Reference-standard definitions and independent blinded review of intended recordings, including uncertain/unavailable outcomes and full-field disagreement resolution.
- Whether evidence supports a narrowly worded learner finding. Current corpus-rating probabilities and engineering admission cannot supply that finding.
- Recording repeatability and an independently justified change criterion before improvement claims. New observations and unfamiliar transfer tasks need a separate declared protocol; identical examples do not supply them.

Actual identity, qualifications, conflicts, guardian authority, consent, use rights and collection authorization must come from people and source records. Models and local passwords cannot establish those facts. The public reference source license is separate from participant permissions. The original desktop continuation recorded no child enrollment, reviewer contact or clinical signoff; that is dated provenance, not a current contact-status assertion.

Before enabling learner findings, archive the reviewed product/content/model/policy versions and the professional decisions, implement any justified changes, and rerun affected engineering checks. Before any public release, establish the actual product-rights/deployment boundary and validate intended-use evidence; the present local tools carry false learner, clinical and production eligibility throughout.
