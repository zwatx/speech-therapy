# App-domain annotation and data preparation

**Prepared / not collected / no reviewers contacted.** Ages 6–12 and the expanded native consumer catalog are the scope. This is an addendum to the [existing SLP handoff](../model/slp-handoff.md), its [content worksheet](../model/practice-content-review.md) and controlled annotation/signoff records, not a new competing approval process. Their historical ages 2–12 and 72-prompt counts do not define this consumer milestone.

## Reference decisions before collection

A qualified reviewer and study owner must choose a narrowly observable task, intended English variety, acceptable dialect/variant productions, age/readiness bands, target localization and reference standard. Use the existing handoff's independent reviewer/adjudicator and signoff process. The [proposed engineering pack](proposed-engineering-pack.md) contains exact dictionary metadata and pending review fields; it is not yet an approved assessment or therapy sequence.

Keep separate annotation fields for expected prompt/variant, observed word/prompt match, heard target production, omitted/altered/expected/indeterminate target under the agreed audible definitions, target observability/location, recording quality, and uncertainty. Original labels and plain-language notes remain immutable. Recording quality, language/dialect difference and unreadiness are not sound errors. A sound observation is not a condition diagnosis. Corpus votes, model predictions, transcripts and dataset membership are not independent clinical truth.

Intelligibility, disorder/condition, movement mechanism, cue appropriateness and treatment response are different tasks. If one is later requested, define a separate qualified reference standard and intended claim; do not auto-derive it from altered sound labels. RGB lip geometry or surface depth cannot establish hidden tongue placement. Reviewed general preparation instructions are distinct from individualized corrective cues.

## Sampling and splits

Recruitment/recording collection requires separate authorization. Prepare a manifest schema with pseudonymous participant ID, household ID (or explicit unknown), age at recording, language/dialect context, prompt/content/variant ID, target occurrence, intended/actual phone/device/OS/microphone, consent/assent/use-rights record versions, session/condition/repetition IDs, processing outcome and reference-review lineage. Keep direct identifiers separately controlled; do not put child data in the content worksheet.

Assign speaker and household groups to development, calibration and untouched evaluation before model use; keep every sibling/session/attempt/repetition in its household partition. If identity or household linkage is unknown, report the inability to guarantee isolation rather than guessing. Record cross-source identity uncertainty and possible duplicates. Reserve an additional unfamiliar-prompt/word split: all variants, spellings and repeated-target occurrences of a held-out lexical word remain grouped. Explicitly declare whether the evaluation jointly holds out speakers/households and words; neither follows from random recording splits. Keep untouched evaluation out of content/model selection.

Ages 6–12 need explicit per-year/readiness and target/context support, including youngest/sparse strata, rather than an aggregate “child” label. Language variety and microphones/conditions need actual measured coverage. Do not exclude ordinary accent differences to manufacture a homogeneous error cohort; preserve unsupported/uncertain scope and state limits on claims. Repeated targets are separate occurrences within correlated words, not independent participants.

## Repeat conditions and denominators

Record every invited/started attempt and completion outcome, including refusal/skip, permission denial, interruption, unusable audio, nonspeech, wrong/extra words, uncertain mapping, unsupported scope, processing failure and rated/unrated results. Preserve retry parent/index and why a retry occurred. Do not select only clearest recordings or favorable model outputs for accuracy/retention denominators. Report eligibility exclusions and missingness separately from processing failures.

Prepare repeat recordings of the same assessed task under predefined session, device/distance/background and adult-support conditions to estimate test/retest variation and condition effects. Add unfamiliar transfer prompts for prospective change evaluation. Fix allowed repeats, prompt presentation/reference playback and coaching rules before collection; these alter the task. Record unsolicited help and condition deviations rather than silently discarding them. Counts of repeats/practice completions are participation, not improved speech.

Dataset size is an unresolved statistical design decision. Use intended precision of agreement, error/abstention/false-correction estimates and test/retest meaningful-change criteria, accounting for clustered children/households and rare errors. An annotation feasibility pilot can estimate reliability and usable yield, then inform a prospective size calculation. No invented quota or claim of power appears here. If agreement/support is inadequate, revise the task or reference source using development evidence and preserve an untouched final cohort.

## Independent review and adjudication

Use at least the existing handoff's two independent qualified reviewers and separately specified adjudicator where required by the chosen task. The owner must verify actual qualification, reference variety and conflicts, not fabricate reviewers or treat automated labels as expert review. Reviewers remain blinded to model/source ratings, other reviewers and development ranking. Randomize/order according to the controlled protocol; preserve independent first exports before comparison.

Record uncertain/unscorable separately; allow absent/uncertain intervals and multiple candidate occurrences. Report inter-rater agreement, paired coverage, disagreements and missingness under the pre-agreed definitions, with age/target/context support. Preserve original responses, adjudicator decisions and reasons separately. Adjudication does not erase disagreement or manufacture perfect reliability. Labels may enter research fitting only after allowed use, task identity and split lineage are checked; labels from reviewed development examples do not become untouched evaluation truth.

## Rights, consent, retention and deletion preparation

Before collection, document the actual source/use permissions for recordings, annotations, reference audio, images, features and derived checkpoints: consent/assent, guardian authority, noncommercial/product restrictions, storage/access, use duration, withdrawal and deletion obligations. Existing public corpus access and local noncommercial research status do not establish product rights. Catalog pronunciation license does not license new pictures, voice recordings or clinical recommendations.

Research contribution is a separate choice from optional consumer profiles/accounts and from using local practice. Do not condition account creation or immediate practice on research upload. Record each contribution's specific permission snapshot; do not treat a profile, microphone permission or general terms click as annotation/training consent. No new collection/upload path is enabled by this preparation.

Choose and record actual local/cloud retention durations, access roles, export/deletion behavior and participant-request handling before collection. Retain only justified media/metadata; preserve existing default temporary native audio cleanup. Map subject lineage into clips/features/labels/checkpoints/reports so withdrawal can quarantine/purge/retrain/re-evaluate as applicable under existing artifact infrastructure. An account reset, local history reset, research withdrawal and cloud deletion have distinct consequences; disclose them plainly. Verify deletion receipts and residual backups where relevant to the chosen storage path; do not claim current local prototypes prove production deletion.

## Ready-for-review outputs and unresolved choices

Engineering provides the actual prompt IDs/variants/indexes, pending worksheet, proposed manifest/split/repetition fields, blinded review requirements, all-attempt accounting and explicit rights/retention questions. Qualified review supplies task definitions, acceptable variants, readiness/support, reference audio/pictures, cue approval and agreement thresholds in the existing controlled records. The owner/statistical reviewer settles size/precision, split strategy, meaningful-change rule and collection authorization. No label, expert approval, diagnosis, participant evidence or finalized collection plan has been supplied in this batch.
