About this preview

For local research only. Results are not pronunciation grades, corrective feedback, a diagnosis, or evidence of improvement. Sound locations may be unverified even when the phrase matches.

Changing the selection, check type, recording, age, or consent discards an active recording, clears the displayed result, and requests cancellation of an active check. We wait for confirmation before enabling another recording or check.
