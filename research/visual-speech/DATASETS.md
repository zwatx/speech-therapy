## Next evidence, after an actually permitted corpus is supplied

1. Extract a small local set through this CLI with item-level rights/provenance and a documented audio clock. Verify real faces, pose, occlusion, identity continuity, and audio/video synchronization manually against the permitted sources. Keep failed/unobservable rows.
2. Preserve speaker-disjoint splits. Online footage and scripted adult corpora offer representation learning candidates; their fluent transcripts are not labels for child correctness. Record age/domain/dialect as known or unknown; do not fill absent labels with “normal.”
3. Define a bounded visible target with appropriate annotation (for example, externally observable lip closure during a timed region). Compare audio-only, RGB-only, and audio+RGB on the same frozen samples, including missing/corrupted modality and time-offset controls. Determine whether visual features add information or merely learn text/speaker/dataset shortcuts.
4. Child ages 6–12, developmental variation, dialect, and speech-sound errors require separately authorized, age-appropriate ground truth and evaluation. Online adult/video benchmark performance cannot establish that validity. A real TrueDepth study is a separate modality with its own missing-data and device calibration issues.

No dataset in this audit established a ready commercial child 6–12 corpus with synchronized audio, RGB, measured depth, visible tooth/tongue annotations, and pronunciation-error truth. This is an evidence gap, not a negative label for those children or sources. The closed AVA speech-presence experiment remains closed.
