# Consumer speech assessment and practice

Speech practice and assessment for children ages 6–12, with optional accounts and caregiver support.

## Language

**Practice prompt**: A word and its intended target-sound position offered for recording and replay. Dictionary pronunciation establishes the intended target, not the child's actual production.

**Assessment attempt**: One requested research recording or explicit preflight rejection for a chosen prompt and declared age. It starts before permission/capture and retains a terminal outcome even when no recording can be submitted. Merely preparing a prompt does not start an attempt. An attempt can remain unassessable.

**Assessment retry**: A new attempt linked to the previous attempt while the same prompt and age setup remain selected. Changing setup or ending the session starts a new chain; a retry does not count as an independent participant or planned repeat.

**Capture quality**: Measurements of the recording, such as duration, volume and clipping. These do not establish speech presence or pronunciation correctness.

**Speech presence**: Evidence that a recording contains speech. It does not establish that the requested word was spoken.

**Prompt match**: Evidence that the recorded speech corresponds to the requested prompt. An assumed canonical sequence is not a verified prompt match.

**Engineering admission**: A permission, capture, speech-evidence and independently recognized-word decision allowing a recording to reach the research model. It does not establish the target sound's correctness or clinical validity.

**Example replay**: A new processing attempt using the identical example recording. It can check reproducibility, but does not measure a new production or speech improvement.

**Planned reassessment**: A new recording obtained under a declared repeat protocol and linked to an earlier assessment. Conditions and model/content versions determine whether observations are comparable; a practice retry or example replay is not independent reassessment.

**Neutral practice**: Manually selected word practice with a reference example and participation record, without an inferred error or individualized corrective cue.

**Research prediction**: A model's distribution over the corpus judgments it was fitted to predict. It is not an approved learner finding.
_Avoid_: Speech score, accuracy, diagnosis

**Learner finding**: A supported, understandable assessment result that may guide a child's practice. Research predictions alone do not establish a finding.

**Participation**: Words or sessions completed in practice. It measures taking part rather than speech improvement.
_Avoid_: Accuracy, mastery, improvement

**Speech improvement**: A reliable change in assessed speech that exceeds the agreed measurement variation. Participation counts alone cannot establish it.

**Unavailable assessment**: An attempt for which the app cannot support a pronunciation finding, including uncertain prompt matching or unsupported evidence.

**Research speech check**: An internal engineering attempt that exercises real recording and model processing while learner findings remain unavailable.
